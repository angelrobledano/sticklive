Stick Live is the app for following rink hockey live. The score updates on its own, with no refreshing, and you get an alert as soon as your team scores.

LIVE
• Today's matches, with the score as it happens.
• If the connection drops in the stands, the app reconnects on its own.

MATCH PAGE
• Goals with scorer and minute, blue and red cards.
• Lineups with each team's starting five.

ALERTS
• Goals, start and full time of the matches you follow.
• For each team, choose: Everything, Key events, Results or Silent.
• Silence at night: late games won't wake you, and the result arrives in the morning.

STANDINGS AND FAVORITES
• Every league table, matchday by matchday.
• Follow teams, leagues and players to keep them close at hand.
• Sign in to take your favorites to all your devices.

COMPETITIONS
Spain's OK Liga and OK Liga Iberdrola, with more competitions added one at a time. The data comes from official sources: federations, leagues and match reports.

Available in Spanish, English, French, Italian and Portuguese, with dark mode.

Stick Live is free and supported by ads.

Stick Live is an independent project and is not affiliated with the Royal Spanish Skating Federation (RFEP), World Skate or any club.
